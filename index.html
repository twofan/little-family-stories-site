Little Family Stories

Short picture-story videos about the world's landmarks, for children aged 3 to 6, in English and Chinese.

What we make

Little Family Stories is an independent, one-family video studio. Each film is a hand-drawn or paper-collage picture story about one place: a tower, a bridge, a canyon, a castle. A story lasts one to two minutes, has a gentle narration and a few true numbers a child can remember, and is made in both English and Chinese.

The films are published on our own channels and are free to watch. There is no app to install, no account to create and nothing to buy.

Where to watch

  • YouTube (Little Family Stories · 逸安爸爸)
  • Bilibili / 哔哩哔哩
  • Also on TikTok, Douyin, Instagram and Threads under the name Little Family Stories.

Our publishing tools

We upload our own finished films to our own channels with a small publishing program that runs on the studio's computer. It signs in to the studio's accounts only, uploads the video, title, description and cover, and reads the view counts of our own videos afterwards. It is not offered to anyone else and never touches other people's accounts. The privacy policy explains exactly what it does with data.

Contact

Write to us on GitHub (a GitHub account is needed; posts are public, so please do not include your child's name, photos or other private details).

亲子小故事

面向 3～6 岁孩子的世界地标图画小故事短片，中英双语。

「亲子小故事」是一个独立的、由一个家庭制作的视频小工作室。每部短片讲一个地方：一座塔、一座桥、一道峡谷、一座城堡，用手绘或拼贴画的方式，配上温和的旁白和几个孩子记得住的真实数字，时长一到两分钟，同时制作英文版和中文版。

短片在我们自己的频道上免费观看，不需要安装应用、注册账号或付费。

观看渠道

  • YouTube（Little Family Stories · 逸安爸爸）
  • 哔哩哔哩
  • 抖音、TikTok、Instagram 和 Threads 上同名账号。

我们的发布工具

我们用一个运行在工作室电脑上的小程序，把自己做好的短片上传到自己的频道：它只登录工作室自己的账号，上传视频、标题、简介和封面，并在之后读取我们自己视频的播放数据。它不提供给其他人使用，也不会接触别人的账号。隐私政策说明了它处理数据的方式。
