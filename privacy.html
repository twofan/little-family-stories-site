Privacy policy

Little Family Stories / 亲子小故事
Updated October 14, 2026

Who we are

Little Family Stories is an independent video studio run by one family. We publish short picture-story videos for children on video platforms. This policy covers this website, our channels and the publishing program we use to upload our own videos.

This website

This website is a few static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical connection information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

Our channels

Our videos are watched on YouTube, Bilibili, TikTok, Douyin, Instagram and Threads. Those platforms collect and process viewer data under their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal information about viewers from them. We only see the aggregate statistics each platform shows every creator, such as view counts, and public comments that viewers choose to post.

Our publishing program (including the YouTube API)

We upload our films with a publishing program that runs on the studio's own computer. It is used only by the studio, for the studio's own accounts. On YouTube it uses the YouTube Data API through Google sign-in to our own channel and asks for permission to upload videos and manage our own channel content (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl), and, if enabled, to read our own channel's analytics (yt-analytics.readonly). With that permission it uploads our video files, titles, descriptions, covers and playlist entries, checks that a video is public, and reads the view counts of our own videos.

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the studio's computer, in a file readable by the studio account alone, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The program does not read, store or share any data about viewers or about other Google users, does not access anyone else's channel, and does not sell, rent or transfer any Google user data. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where another platform offers an equivalent official interface, the program uses it in the same way and for the same purpose: our own account, our own videos, our own statistics.

Children

Our videos are made for children, and the platforms label them accordingly. We do not collect personal information from children, we run no app or service that children sign in to, and this website has no forms. Parents who contact us should not send their child's personal information.

Contact and support

If you contact us through GitHub, we receive what you choose to write and use it only to respond. Posts there are public and subject to GitHub's terms and privacy practices.

Changes

Any change to these practices will be described here with an updated date.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日。适用于「亲子小故事 / Little Family Stories」。

我们是谁。亲子小故事是由一个家庭运营的独立视频小工作室，在视频平台上发布面向儿童的图画小故事短片。本政策涵盖本网站、我们的频道，以及我们用来上传自己视频的发布程序。

本网站。本网站是托管在 GitHub Pages 上的几个静态页面，没有 Cookie、统计、广告或追踪脚本。托管方 GitHub 可能依据其隐私声明处理 IP 地址等技术连接信息。

我们的频道。短片在 YouTube、哔哩哔哩、TikTok、抖音、Instagram 和 Threads 上观看，这些平台依照各自的隐私政策收集和处理观众数据；我们不会从平台获得观众的个人信息，只能看到平台向每位创作者展示的汇总数据（如播放量）以及观众自己公开发表的评论。

我们的发布程序（含 YouTube API）。我们用一个运行在工作室自己电脑上的发布程序上传短片，它只供工作室使用，只操作工作室自己的账号。在 YouTube 上，它通过 Google 登录到我们自己的频道，使用 YouTube Data API，申请的权限是上传视频和管理我们自己频道的内容（youtube.upload 与 youtube.force-ssl），以及在启用时读取我们自己频道的数据分析（yt-analytics.readonly）。凭这些权限，它上传我们的视频文件、标题、简介、封面和播放列表，检查视频是否已公开，并读取我们自己视频的播放量。

Google 登录凭证只保存在工作室电脑上、仅工作室账户可读的文件中，可随时在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销。该程序不读取、保存或分享任何关于观众或其他 Google 用户的数据，不访问任何他人的频道，也不出售、出租或转让任何 Google 用户数据。我们对来自 Google API 信息的使用遵守《Google API 服务用户数据政策》及其「有限使用」要求。

其他平台若提供同类官方接口，程序以同样的方式和目的使用：只针对自己的账号、自己的视频、自己的数据。

儿童。我们的短片面向儿童，平台会相应标注。我们不收集儿童的个人信息，没有任何供儿童登录的应用或服务，本网站也没有任何表单。联系我们的家长请勿发送孩子的个人信息。

联系与支持。通过 GitHub 联系我们时，我们只接收您自愿写下的内容并仅用于回复；留言公开可见，适用 GitHub 的条款与隐私规则。

变更。这些做法如有变化，会在本页说明并更新日期。
